Thai restaurants in Edinburgh

Edinburgh's Thai restaurant scene is concentrated around the city centre, with popular spots on the Royal Mile, in the New Town, and in neighbourhoods like Bruntsfield and Morningside. The city is known for its Thai street food culture, including the beloved Ting Thai group and traditional eateries serving authentic dishes from across Thailand.
