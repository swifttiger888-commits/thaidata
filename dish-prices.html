What the data tells us

  • Delivery markups average 20-23% across all dishes. Spring Rolls have the highest markup at +23%; Sticky Rice the lowest at +18%.
  • London is the most expensive city for Thai dining, with an average dine-in price of £12.08 and delivery of £14.58.
  • Leeds has the highest delivery markup at +23.0%, despite having the lowest absolute prices. Customers in Leeds pay the largest relative premium for delivery.
  • Manchester and Bristol are the most affordable cities among the five surveyed for dine-in Thai food.
  • The markup is rarely disclosed — customers paying £16.90 for Massaman Curry on a delivery app may not know it's £13.95 in the restaurant.

Methodology & Limitations

Prices were collected in June 2026 from a sample of 3-5 popular Thai restaurants per city. Dine-in prices come from official restaurant websites or Google Business Profile menu photos. Delivery prices come from Deliveroo, Uber Eats, and Just Eat menu listings. Where a restaurant does not offer a specific dish, it was excluded from the average. Prices exclude optional service charges and delivery fees.

This is a manual survey, not an automated scrape. Coverage will expand as we collect more data. Full methodology report.
