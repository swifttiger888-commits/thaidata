London's Thai food scene is bigger and more diverse than any other UK city. With 273 FSA-listed businesses spanning every borough from Westminster to Greenwich, it is not a single scene -- it is dozens of overlapping ones.

In Soho and Chinatown, you will find late-night canteens serving green curry until midnight. In Shoreditch and Hackney, independent Thai cafes do inventive takes on classics alongside street-food stalls at weekend markets. In Kensington and Wimbledon, long-established family restaurants have been serving loyal customers for two decades. And in the outer boroughs, neighbourhood Thai takeaways quietly serve some of the best food in the city.

This guide breaks London down into its dining neighbourhoods. Each section includes real FSA hygiene data, our top-rated picks, and notes on which chains operate where. The data comes from the Food Standards Agency's most recent inspections -- every rating is verifiable and independently audited.

Where to Start

Quick lunch

Head to Soho and Chinatown for fast, reliable Thai -- Busaba, Banana Tree, and several walk-in canteens on Lisle Street.

Date night

Kensington or Marylebone for polished service and wine lists. Try Patara or a local independent on Kensington Church Street.

Group dinner

Shoreditch and Spitalfields has the highest density with room for big tables. Rosa's Thai on Commercial Street is a solid group choice.

Family takeaway

Putney and Wimbledon has the highest average FSA rating in London (★4.8/5) -- these are community staples.

Pre-theatre

South Bank or Islington -- both near major theatres and packed with pre-show dining options.

Foodie exploration

Hackney and Dalston for off-the-beaten-path finds. Broadway Market and Kingsland Road have excellent independent Thai spots.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the top-rated Thai restaurant in London?

London has 151 restaurants with a perfect 5/5 FSA hygiene rating. Top-rated spots include Anna's Thai, Arya Thai, Bai Thai.

How many Thai restaurants are in London?

London has 273 FSA-listed Thai businesses in total -- 223 dine-in restaurants and 50 takeaways. That is 22.0% of all Thai businesses in the UK.

Which Thai chains operate in London?

Major chains with London locations include Thai Square (9) and Busaba (7) and Patara (5) and Thai Express (3) and Giggling Squid (3). The vast majority of London's Thai restaurants are independent.

What is the best area for Thai food in London?

Shoreditch and Spitalfields has the highest concentration with 13 restaurants. Putney and Wimbledon has the highest average FSA rating at ★4.8/5.

Is London's Thai food scene competitive?

With 273 businesses serving 8.97 million people, London has roughly 4.5 Thai restaurants per 100,000 residents -- slightly below the national average. This suggests room for growth, particularly in underserved outer boroughs.

Are there more dine-in Thai restaurants or takeaways in London?

Dine-in restaurants dominate at 223 (82% of the total), with 50 takeaways making up the remainder.
