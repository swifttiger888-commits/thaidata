About Thai Garden

Welcome to Thai Garden, where we bring the vibrant flavours of Thailand to the heart of Leeds. Our menu celebrates the balance of sweet, sour, salty, and spicy that defines authentic Thai cooking — from our signature Phad Thai to slow-cooked Massaman curry, every dish is made fresh to order using ingredients sourced directly from trusted suppliers.

Whether you're joining us for a relaxed lunch, a celebratory dinner, or ordering takeaway for the family, our team is committed to making every meal memorable. We also offer a dedicated vegetarian menu and can cater for most dietary requirements with advance notice.
