Dine-In or Takeaway

Of 1,245 Thai businesses, 1,002 (80.5%) are dine-in restaurants and 243 (19.5%) are takeaways. Each city guide breaks down the split locally.

Dine-in restaurants ★

Full-service Thai restaurants offering sit-down dining. These make up the majority of the market and tend to have the highest hygiene ratings. Most are independent, family-run operations.

Thai takeaways 🛍

Takeaway-focused businesses, often operating from smaller premises. Particularly common in cities with large student populations and suburban areas where delivery demand is high.

About This Directory

How the data is collected, verified, and kept current.

Data source: All ratings come from the Food Standards Agency (FHRS) and Food Standards Scotland (FHIS) -- official hygiene inspection scores from local authority visits.

Coverage: We track every FSA-listed business in England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland that self-identifies as Thai cuisine. This includes restaurants, takeaways, cafes, and mobile caterers.

Updates: The directory refreshes weekly from the FSA API. Each restaurant page shows its inspection date, so you know exactly how fresh the rating is.

Accuracy: Business names and types come from the FSA register. Some businesses may have changed name or closed since their last inspection. Always call ahead.
