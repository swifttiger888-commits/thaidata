How this menu is priced

  • Soups: choose your protein — prawns or squid £6.80, chicken £6.50, mushrooms, tofu or vegetables £5.00.
  • Rad Kaw dishes are served over steamed jasmine rice: vegetables or tofu £11.00, chicken or pork £11.95, beef, prawn, squid, crispy chicken or crispy belly pork £12.95, duck £13.50; add a fried egg +£1.50.
  • Curries: tofu or vegetables £8.95, chicken or pork £10.00, beef, prawn, squid, crispy chicken or crispy belly pork £11.00, duck £12.00; rice not included.
  • Noodle & rice dishes: tofu or vegetables £10.00, chicken or pork £12.50, prawn or beef £13.50.
  • Some dishes can be made gluten free or vegan — please speak to staff when ordering.
