Key Findings — June 2026

  • London has 411 Thai businesses — 26% of the UK total. 323 dine-in, 88 takeaways. But it's only 10th in per-capita density.
  • Swansea is the UK's most Thai-dense city at 5.7/100k, followed by Norwich (5.4) and Bath (5.1)
  • Scotland has 70 Thai businesses across 21 council areas — Edinburgh leads with 24, Glasgow has 8
  • Birmingham severely underserved — just 1.5/100k for the UK's 2nd-largest city (1,166,000 pop, only 17 Thai businesses)
  • Glasgow & Cheltenham share the highest average hygiene rating at ★5.0
  • Giggling Squid, Busaba, Chaophraya, Patara, Sukhothai and Zaap are now included — chains previously missed because their names don't contain "Thai"

Data Source & Methodology — June 2026

Primary source: Food Standards Agency (FSA) — api.ratings.food.gov.uk. Official UK government data. Every food business in the UK must register. Free, public, no API key needed.

What's included:
• All FSA-registered establishments with "Thai" in their business name — both FHRS (England, Wales, NI) and FHIS (Scotland) schemes
• Business types: Restaurant/Cafe/Canteen (counted as "dine-in") and Takeaway/Sandwich Shop
• Known Thai chains that don't have "Thai" in their name: Giggling Squid, Busaba, Chaophraya, Patara, Sukhothai, Zaap
• Only businesses with a completed inspection (122 awaiting first inspection excluded)
• Scottish "Pass" ratings mapped to ★5, "Improvement Required" to ★2

Limitations (and what we've done about them):
• ✓ Chains without "Thai" in name: Giggling Squid, Busaba, Chaophraya, Patara, Sukhothai, and Zaap are now included via separate API queries
• ✓ Scotland missing: Now included via the FHIS scheme API — 70 businesses across 21 council areas
• ✓ Uninspected premises: Filtered out (122 removed). Only rated businesses are shown
• ~ Duplicate registrations: A business operating from multiple units (e.g. dine-in + dark kitchen) may appear twice. This inflates counts slightly — estimated <5% impact
• ~ Ratings ≠ food quality: FSA scores reflect food hygiene compliance, not taste, service, or value
• ~ Business name matching: Some Thai restaurants may still be missed if they use names that don't contain "Thai" and aren't in our chain list. We believe coverage is now >95%
• ~ City boundaries: Data is grouped by FSA Local Authority areas. Some LA areas cover wider regions than their named city (e.g. Cornwall, North Yorkshire)

Last updated: 3 June 2026. FSA data refreshes daily.
