Key Findings

  • London dominates with 367 Thai businesses — 51% of the UK total — but it's only 10th in per-capita density
  • Swansea is the UK's most Thai-dense city at 6.9 restaurants per 100k people, followed by Norwich (6.8) and Manchester (5.5)
  • Birmingham — the UK's second-largest city — has just 1.6 Thai businesses per 100k. Massive undersupply for a 1.1M+ population
  • Brighton has the highest-rated Thai restaurants (★4.8 avg), while Huddersfield is lowest among major cities (★3.2)
  • 52% of cities with populations over 200,000 have fewer than 3 Thai restaurants per 100k — potential openings for new operators
  • Scotland & NI excluded — these use a different inspection scheme (FHIS). Estimated ~120-150 additional Thai businesses

Data Source & Methodology

Source: Food Standards Agency (FSA) Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (FHRS) API — api.ratings.food.gov.uk. Data fetched June 2026. Free government data, no registration required.

Methodology: We queried the FHRS API for all establishments with "Thai" in their registered business name, filtered to BusinessType "Restaurant/Cafe/Canteen" (dine-in) and "Takeaway/Sandwich Shop" (takeaway). Results grouped by Local Authority area, then mapped to city/metro areas. London combines all 32 boroughs + City of London.

Limitations:
• Only captures businesses with "Thai" in their FSA-registered name — misses some chains (Giggling Squid, Busaba, Patara) and restaurants using non-Thai trading names
• Scotland & Northern Ireland use separate inspection schemes (FHIS) — not included here. The existing UK overview estimates ~120 Scottish Thai restaurants
• FSA data includes all registered food premises, including those awaiting first inspection (shown as "Awaiting Inspection")
• Ratings reflect food hygiene compliance, not food quality or taste
• A single business with multiple FSA registrations (e.g., delivery-only dark kitchen + dine-in) may appear as separate entries

Last Updated: 3 June 2026. Data reflects the FSA register at time of collection. FSA updates daily.
