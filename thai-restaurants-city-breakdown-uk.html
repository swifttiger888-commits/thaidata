About This Data

How we collect, verify, and present city-level Thai restaurant data.

Data source

All ratings come from the Food Standards Agency (FHRS) and Food Standards Scotland (FHIS). Every rating is a hygiene inspection score from an official visit.

Coverage

We track all FSA-registered businesses in England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland that identify as Thai cuisine. This includes restaurants, takeaways, and cafes.

Update frequency

City data refreshes weekly from the FSA API. Each city guide shows a date badge so you know exactly how fresh the data is.

Accuracy

Chain detection uses keyword matching against known UK Thai chains. Independent businesses may share names with chains in other regions. Data quibbles? Email us.
