Overview

"Thai Elephant" is not a unified chain in the UK, but rather a collection of approximately 5-6 independent restaurants operating under the same name across England. Each location is separately owned and managed with its own website, branding, and menu. The restaurants are located in Twyford (Berkshire), West Wickham/Coney Hall (Greater London), Richmond (London), Leamington Spa (Warwickshire), Street (Somerset), and Peacehaven/East Sussex (Thai Elephant Two). There is no evidence of corporate ownership or franchising connecting these establishments.

Despite their independence, the restaurants share a common positioning around authentic Thai cuisine. Most emphasise family-run operations, traditional recipes, and classic Thai decor. The Leamington Spa location, for example, highlights "over 20 years of family experience" and staff in traditional Thai costumes, while the Twyford location showcases "many years of experience at five-star fine dining establishments in Thailand" among its chefs. Menus across locations feature standard Thai dishes including curries (green, red, Panang), stir-fries, Pad Thai, Tom Yum soup, and sharing-style starters, priced in the moderate ££ range with mains typically £10-18.

Hygiene ratings vary considerably across the independent locations. Based on the five ratings discovered through FSA/food hygiene data (Leamington Spa: 5/5, Aug 2023; Somerset: 5/5, Nov 2023; Richmond: 3/5, Feb 2025; Twyford: 3/5, Apr 2025; West Wickham: 2/5, Jun 2025), the average is approximately 3.6 out of 5. This variation reflects the independent nature of each operation. Diners should check individual location ratings when choosing where to eat.
