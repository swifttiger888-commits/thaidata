Overview

Wagamama is one of the UK's most recognisable pan-Asian restaurant chains, founded by Hong Kong-born British restaurateur Alan Yau in Bloomsbury, London in 1992. Inspired by the fast-paced ramen bars of Tokyo, Wagamama introduced a novel dining concept to the UK: communal bench seating, open kitchens, and freshly prepared Asian-inspired dishes served as soon as they were ready. The name "Wagamama" is a Japanese word meaning "self-indulgent" or "doing things your own way," reflecting the brand's ethos of individuality and breaking from traditional dining conventions.

While primarily Japanese-inspired, Wagamama's menu features several Thai-influenced dishes that have become customer favourites. These include multiple Pad Thai variations (chicken, prawn, and vegan Yasai Pad Thai made with rice noodles, peanuts and tamarind), Thai Beef Salad, and coconut-based curries such as the Tofu Raisukaree Curry and Chicken Itame (lemongrass coconut curry). In 2024, the chain highlighted demand for "classic dishes such as chicken katsu curry, pad thai and bang bang cauliflower" as drivers of growth. The menu spans ramen, teppanyaki, donburi rice bowls, katsu curry, sides, and a dedicated kids' menu, with extensive vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options.

The chain has been under The Restaurant Group ownership since 2018 and was acquired by Apollo Global Management in 2023 in a deal worth over £700 million. Wagamama is the flagship brand of the group, representing over 65% of group valuation. In 2024, Wagamama announced plans to open 10 new UK sites (up from six in 2023), with a longer-term plan to grow from 161 to 200-220 UK restaurants. The chain also has a significant international presence with 50+ franchised restaurants across Europe, the Middle East, and the United States. The 2024 launch of the Wagamama Soul Club loyalty app exceeded 2 million active users by mid-2025, driving a 14% rise in repeat visit rates and supporting the brand's shift to experience-led, data-driven customer retention.
