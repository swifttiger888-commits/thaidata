Overview

Thai House is a London-based Thai street food business operating 5 market stalls and food hall locations across central London. Founded by Andrey after a transformative trip to Thailand in 2010 where he met Chef Nut at a hotel restaurant, the business began as a single food stall at Lower Marsh Market in Waterloo and has since expanded to multiple locations. Chef Nut brings over 15 years of culinary experience and generations of authentic Thai recipes passed down from her mother, giving the business a strong claim to authenticity in a competitive market.

The business operates five locations: Lower Marsh Market (Waterloo), Tachbrook Street Market (Pimlico), Strutton Ground Market (Victoria), Market Place Vauxhall, and Market Place St Pauls. The first three are weekday lunch markets (Monday-Friday), while the Market Place locations operate seven days a week with extended evening hours. Thai House is also available for delivery via Deliveroo and Just Eat, and offers call-and-collect pickup. The menu features classic Thai dishes including curries (Massaman, green, red, yellow), noodles, and vegan options, prepared fresh with bold spices.

Thai House positions itself as one of London's most authentic Thai street food vendors, emphasizing fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, and the health benefits of Thai cuisine. The brand has cultivated a loyal following, including attention from London food bloggers on Instagram. The Market Place St Pauls location holds a 5-star Food Hygiene Rating from the FSA (inspected June 2025). The business appears to be in a growth phase, having expanded from a single market stall to five locations across London, suggesting increasing demand and successful brand recognition.
