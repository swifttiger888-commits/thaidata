Overview

"Baan Thai" is not a unified restaurant chain in the UK. The name is used by multiple independent Thai restaurants operating across the country. "Baan" (บ้าน) means "home" in Thai, making "Baan Thai" a natural and popular naming choice meaning "Thai home" - hence its widespread use among independent Thai restaurant operators. Research found at least seven distinct restaurants using this name, each with separate ownership, websites, and no apparent corporate connection.

The most formally documented was Baan Thai Restaurant (UK) Limited (company number 04641402), incorporated in January 2003 and dissolved in June 2017, which operated in Burton-on-Trent. However, the Burton restaurant continues trading under the same name despite the company's dissolution. Other independently operated "Baan Thai" restaurants were identified in Hartlepool, Cambridge, Eltham (London), Kentish Town (London), Ryde (Isle of Wight), and Barton-le-Clay (Bedfordshire). None show evidence of shared ownership or franchise arrangements.

Hygiene ratings vary significantly across locations, reflecting their independent status. The Eltham location achieves top-tier standards (rated 5/5 in December 2025), while Cambridge, Hartlepool, Ryde, and Burton-on-Trent hold solid 4/5 ratings. The Kentish Town location received a 0/5 rating in March 2024, highlighting the inconsistency that comes with independent operation rather than chain-standardised practices. Price points across locations are typically mid-range (££), with main dishes generally priced between £11-£20.
