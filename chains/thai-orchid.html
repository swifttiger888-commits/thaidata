Overview

Thai Orchid is the trading name used by approximately 17 independently operated Thai restaurants across the UK. Unlike a conventional franchise chain, these restaurants appear to be largely autonomous, family-run businesses that share the "Thai Orchid" brand name. There is no central chain website or unified booking system; each location operates its own website, menu, and social media presence. In October 2023, "Thai Orchid UK Restaurants Ltd" was incorporated at Companies House (director: Dr Supriyo Roy), suggesting a possible move toward more centralised management, though the extent of its operational control over individual restaurants is unclear.

The restaurants position themselves as authentic Thai dining establishments, with many emphasising that their chefs trained in Thailand. Menus typically feature classic Thai dishes such as green curry, Pad Thai, Tom Yum soup, and Massaman curry, with pricing in the mid-range bracket (starters £5-£12, mains £10-£19, set banquets £18-£40 per person). Individual locations trace their origins to different periods: Thai Orchid Maidenhead claims to have served since 1988, Thai Orchid Preston opened in 2000, and the Banbury location (Thai Orchid Restaurant Limited) was incorporated in 1998. Most locations offer dine-in, takeaway, and delivery through partners like Just Eat, Uber Eats, and Deliveroo. Several also feature Sunday buffet lunches.

Food hygiene ratings across the 17 tracked locations vary considerably. According to Flux IoT data (June 2026), the average rating is 4.1/5, which sits below the national average of 4.5. Four locations hold the top 5-star rating (Maidenhead, Battle, Solihull, and Banbury), while the Scarborough location received a 1-star rating in March 2026. Other notable ratings include Preston (4/5, November 2025), Salisbury (4/5, November 2024), Glasgow (Pass under Scottish FHIS scheme, June 2022), Southborough (3/5, June 2025), Maidstone (3/5, February 2025), and Whitstable (3/5, September 2025). One operator, Thai Orchid Southborough Ltd, was dissolved in September 2024, though the Southborough restaurant continues trading under different ownership.
