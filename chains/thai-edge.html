Overview

Thai Edge is a Thai restaurant chain that was founded in 2000 by husband-and-wife team Harish and Nancy Nathwani, launching its first location in Birmingham's then-new Brindleyplace development. The chain grew to encompass four UK locations — Birmingham, Leeds, Cardiff, and Bristol — and built a solid reputation for consistent, authentic Thai cuisine served in modern, upscale surroundings that deliberately avoided stereotypical Thai restaurant decor. In 2013, the business celebrated what was described as an "ever-expanding restaurant empire" with coverage in the Birmingham Post highlighting its popularity with sports stars and celebrities.

The chain's decline began around 2017 when the founding couple decided to retire. The Birmingham flagship closed in January 2017 after 16 years and was reborn as Siamais (a bar-restaurant concept operated by their son Nishil Nathwani). The Cardiff location closed due to St David's Centre redevelopment, and the Bristol site was converted into an Aluna cocktail bar, also by Nishil. The Leeds restaurant was sold to a third party which now holds sole rights to the Thai Edge name. As of 2025, only the Leeds location remains, and its current operational status is unclear — while it received a 4/5 food hygiene rating on 23 July 2025, multiple restaurant directories list it as "permanently closed" or "closed," and its presence on delivery platforms has been withdrawn (Uber Eats shows it closed as of October 2025). The parent company THAI EDGE LIMITED (incorporated 2003) remains active at Companies House.
