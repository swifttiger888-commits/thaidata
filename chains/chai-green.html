Overview

Chai Green (trading as Chai Green 1823) is a UK-based Halal cafe chain specialising in South Asian street food and breakfast dishes. Founded in Birmingham in 2020 by Zill-E-Hasnain Siddiqui, the chain takes its name and branding from the story of the 1823 discovery of tea in Assam, India. The company's first location opened on Alum Rock Road in Birmingham, and it has since expanded to multiple cities across England and into Scotland and Wales.

The chain positions itself in the fast-casual Halal cafe sector, offering an all-day breakfast menu that includes traditional South Asian options (Desi breakfast, halwa puri, karak chai) alongside Western-fusion dishes (masala omelette bagels, street burgers, nachos with keema). Chai Green has gained recognition for its Instagram-worthy interior design and was awarded "Best Restaurant of the Year" at the 2022 Birmingham Awards and "Best Cafe of the Year" at the 2023 Central England Awards. The brand is also a member of the British Franchise Association and actively franchises its concept with a stated ambition of reaching 30 UK stores.

However, the chain's expansion has not been without challenges. The Watford location, which opened in January 2025 as the brand's eighth site, closed less than a year later in November 2025 and was put on the market. Additionally, Companies House records show a "First Gazette notice for compulsory strike-off" was issued for CHAI GREEN GROUP LTD on 31 March 2026, indicating potential financial or compliance difficulties. Despite these setbacks, the chain continues to list new locations in Manchester and Glasgow on its website, suggesting ongoing expansion efforts.
