Overview

"Thai Terrace" is not a unified restaurant chain in the UK. Rather, it is a name used by several independent Thai restaurants operating in different locations. The most prominent is The Thai Terrace in Guildford, Surrey, which is operated by "The Thai Terrace Limited" (company number 03686244), incorporated on 21 December 1998. This restaurant is a long-established fixture in Guildford, located on the 7th floor of Castle Car Park, offering a "stunning location" with "amazing views over Guildford and the hills beyond" according to Harden's Restaurant Guide. The restaurant features contemporary Thai dining with seating for up to 200 guests, a stylish bar area, and a popular Sunset Lounge terrace.

A separate and unrelated Kensington Thai Terrace operates at 14 Wrights Lane, London W8 6TF, in the South Kensington area. This restaurant has its own website (kensingtonthaiterrace.co.uk) and branding, and is not connected to the Guildford operation. A third venue at 9 Thames Street, Windsor has been operating as "Brown Rice" (an all-you-can-eat Thai buffet) since 2019, though still trading from the same address. Each venue has its own management, website, and distinct identity, with hygiene ratings varying significantly between locations (Guildford: 5/5, Kensington: 3/5, Windsor: 4/5 as Brown Rice).
