Overview

Itsu is a British chain of East Asian-inspired fast-casual restaurants and food-to-go shops founded by Julian Metcalfe (co-founder of Pret a Manger) in Chelsea, London in 1997. Inspired by a trip to Tokyo where Metcalfe observed the precision and artistry of Japanese bento boxes, Itsu was created to bring "beautiful fast food" to the UK market. The brand name derives from the Japanese word meaning "whenever," reflecting the company's commitment to convenience, consistency, and affordability.

The Itsu menu spans sushi, sashimi, hot rice bowls, noodle soups, gyoza, and bao buns, with notable Thai-influenced offerings including Thai chicken curry, Thai salmon curry, Thai king prawn curry, Thai noodles (udon), and little Thai chicken curry bowls. Itsu's key positioning centres on health: every restaurant has its own kitchen team preparing dishes that are steamed, never fried. The menu is 40% plant-based, protein-packed, fibre-rich, and the majority of meals are under 500 calories. The company also operates a growing grocery division with over 70 products stocked in 56,000+ supermarkets across Europe.

In terms of expansion, Itsu opened six new UK and France shops in 2024 with a seventh on Oxford Street in December 2024, plus a flagship site at Manchester's Trafford Centre in early 2025. The chain has expanded to more than 80 UK sites and plans to double its estate over time, with international franchised locations in Brussels Airport, Paris, and Amsterdam's Schiphol Airport. The chain is also diversifying beyond the high street through corporate catering, stadium partnerships (including Emirates Stadium hospitality boxes), and workplace delivery platforms. Three regional franchise stores in Edinburgh, Aberdeen, and Reading closed in early 2025 after the franchisee's pre-pack sale, but the overall trajectory remains growth-focused.
