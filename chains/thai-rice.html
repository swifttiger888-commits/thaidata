Overview

Thai Rice (also known as Thai Rice Group) is a family-owned UK restaurant chain founded in 2002 on Portobello Road in Notting Hill, London. The group currently operates three restaurant branches in London: Thai Rice Portobello Road (the original flagship), Thai Rice Maida Vale, and Thai Rice Harrow Road. In addition to its restaurants, the group has diversified into three affiliated pubs and clubs outside London: Thai Rice Club in Maidstone, The Queens Head in Newark, and The City Arms Inn in Canterbury.

The chain positions itself as an authentic Thai dining destination, using traditional family recipes and fresh ingredients to produce classic Thai dishes. The menu covers the full range of Thai cuisine including curries (red, green, massaman, panang), stir-fries, noodles (Pad Thai, Pad See Ew), Thai salads (Som Tum, Larb), grilled dishes, and vegetarian options. Pricing is mid-range, with mains typically between £11–£17 and lunch deals available for under £15, making it an accessible option for everyday dining. The restaurants aim to deliver "excellent cuisine at a reasonable price" with a welcoming ambience, contemporary decor, and friendly service.

In terms of food hygiene, Thai Rice Portobello Road holds a 4/5 rating (inspected February 2025 by Kensington & Chelsea), while Thai Rice Maida Vale holds a 5/5 rating (inspected May 2024 by Westminster). Thai Rice Harrow Road was last inspected in July 2021 and its current rating is not publicly visible in detail, though the inspection record shows "Very Good" for food handling, "Generally Satisfactory" for cleanliness, and "Good" for management. Across its two most recently rated locations, the chain averages approximately 4–4.5/5 for hygiene.
