Overview

"Thai Garden" is not a UK restaurant chain. Extensive research across Companies House, FSA hygiene records, food industry publications, and web directories found no evidence of a unified chain, franchise network, or multi-site group operating under the "Thai Garden" name in the UK. Instead, the name is used by multiple independent, unaffiliated restaurants across the country that share no corporate ownership, central management, or brand coordination.

The most prominent UK restaurants operating under the "Thai Garden" name include:

- The Thai Garden Cafe — 93 Trafalgar Road, Greenwich, London SE10 9TS. An award-winning, family-run independent restaurant with a 4.5/5 rating on TripAdvisor (79 reviews). It serves dine-in, takeaway, and offers catering for events and dinner parties. It has a food hygiene rating of 5 (very good) from the Royal Borough of Greenwich Council. The restaurant is positioned as BYOB (Bring Your Own Bottle) with outdoor seating, and is popular with local Greenwich residents and visitors to the nearby Cutty Sark and Royal Museums Greenwich.

- Thai Garden — 100 West Street, Bedminster, Bristol BS3 3LR. An independent restaurant with a 4.2/5 rating on TripAdvisor (110 reviews). It holds a food hygiene rating of 5 (very good) from Bristol City Council, last inspected on 29 October 2024. Reviewers describe it as a "genuine family restaurant" with a large menu and reasonable prices (two starters and two mains circa £30-35 as of recent reviews).

- Thai Garden — 3 Market Hill, Rothwell, Northamptonshire NN14 6EP. A small independent Thai restaurant with a food hygiene rating checked in July 2025.

- Thai Garden Restaurant Ltd — A dissolved UK company (Company number 11348045) registered at 309 Hoe Street, Walthamstow, London E17 9BG. Incorporated 7 May 2018 and dissolved 30 July 2019, suggesting a short-lived single restaurant operation.

Additionally, there is a Thai Garden Restaurant in Blanchardstown, Dublin, Ireland (thaigarden.ie), which is a separate, well-established independent restaurant operating since 2008 — but this is in the Republic of Ireland, not the UK. Numerous "Thai Garden" restaurants also exist in the United States (New York, California, Illinois, Texas, Oregon, Washington, and others), all independently operated.
