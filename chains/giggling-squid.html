Overview

Giggling Squid is one of the UK's largest Thai restaurant chains, founded in 2002 by husband-and-wife team Andy and Pranee Laurillard in Brighton. The brand began in a tiny fisherman's cottage in The Lanes (still their Brighton restaurant today) and has grown to 53 UK locations as of 2026. The chain is known for its "Thai tapas" concept, particularly at lunchtime, where diners can share multiple small plates rather than ordering a single curry. The menu is designed to be accessible to UK palates — Pranee oversees all menu development — with authentic Thai flavours presented in a "sanitised" way that avoids intimidating ingredients or overly challenging flavours.

A key element of Giggling Squid's brand is its deliberate departure from traditional Thai restaurant aesthetics. Co-founder Andy Laurillard, a former Unilever and Coca-Cola marketer, made the conscious decision to create a neutral, welcoming interior style rather than the ornate carved-wood temple decor common in Thai restaurants. This, combined with their strategy of targeting smaller affluent market towns (Horsham, Marlow, Reigate, Berkhamsted) rather than competing in major city centres, has allowed the chain to carve out a distinctive niche. The brand places strong emphasis on lunchtime trade, where female diners reportedly outnumber men by five to one, and all pastes and sauces are made fresh on-site at each restaurant.

The chain has demonstrated consistent growth over the past two decades, expanding from 13 sites in 2015 to 50+ by 2025, with new openings planned in York and Leeds. In 2015, the business secured investment from the Business Growth Fund (BGF) to accelerate expansion. Giggling Squid also boasts exceptional food hygiene standards — as of June 2026, 100% of its 53 rated UK locations hold a perfect 5-star Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (FHRS) score, placing it well above the national average.
