Overview

Rosa's Thai (formerly branded as Rosa's Thai Cafe) is one of the UK's largest and most successful Thai restaurant chains, founded in 2008 by husband-and-wife team Saiphin and Alex Moore. The first restaurant opened on Hanbury Street in London's Spitalfields, taking over a former British caff called "Rosa's Cafe" — the name was kept out of respect for the site's history (and reportedly because they couldn't afford new signage). From these humble beginnings, the chain has grown to approximately 41 locations across the UK as of 2026, with 26 in London and 15+ across major cities in England, Scotland, and Wales.

The chain is built around founder Saiphin Moore's family recipes from her upbringing on a mountain farm in Khao Kho, northern Thailand. The brand differentiates itself through a commitment to authenticity — importing key ingredients directly from Thailand, including owning farmland in Pattalung province that produces all the lemongrass (40,000kg/year) and galangal (32,000kg/year) the chain requires. Rosa's Thai also operates its own curry paste factory in Thailand, producing fresh, all-natural curry paste that is shipped to UK restaurants. Despite this authenticity, the chain maintains an accessible price point with most dishes priced under £10, positioning it as affordable casual dining.

The chain has received significant private equity investment, including a majority stake acquisition by TriSpan LLP in 2018 to fund expansion. Rosa's Thai has consistently been ranked among the UK's fastest-growing restaurant chains — placing 6th in the AlixPartners Growth Company Index 2019. The brand has also expanded internationally, with its first overseas location opening in Dubai, UAE. The chain boasts an exceptional food hygiene record, with 100% of its UK locations holding a perfect 5-star rating from the Food Standards Agency.
