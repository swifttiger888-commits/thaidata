Overview

Thai Street Cafe is not a formal restaurant chain. Rather, it is a name used by at least four independent, unconnected Thai restaurants operating across England. Despite sharing the same name, these establishments have separate ownership, different menus, distinct branding, and no corporate or operational ties. The most prominent of these is the Newmarket location in Suffolk, which operates under a registered limited company — THAI STREET CAFE LIMITED (company number 07010852), incorporated on 7 September 2009 and directed by Joseph Baron and Christopher James Gray.

The four known "Thai Street Cafe" locations are:

1. Newmarket, Suffolk — thaistreetcafe.co.uk — 26-28 High Street, CB8 8LB. Operated by THAI STREET CAFE LIMITED. Serves authentic Thai street food with a bar and cocktail menu. Open lunch and dinner Tue-Sat. Prices: ££ (mains £11–£17). FSA hygiene rating: 5/5 (inspected 1 November 2025).

2. Aldeburgh, Suffolk — thaistreetcafealdeburgh.com — 106-108 High Street (DP's Bar courtyard), IP15 5AB. Run by Eliza Stephens. Described as a "hidden gem" serving authentic Thai street food with local seafood specials. Open Tue-Sat evenings. Prices: £££ (mains £14–£18, starters £12–£15). FSA hygiene rating: 4/5 (inspected 27 March 2026).

3. Southampton — 3 Pound Tree Road, SO14 1ND. Run by chef Orawan Walton ("Katie"). Opened February 2019. Small, popular eatery near West Quay shopping centre known for generous portions at low prices. Open daily. Prices: £ (mains £9–£10). FSA hygiene rating: 5/5 (inspected 16 July 2025). Delivery via Deliveroo.

4. Ruskington, Lincolnshire — thaistreetcaferuskington.co.uk — 31 High Street North, NG34 9DY. Family-run cafe with both dining area and coffee shop. Serves Thai street food and specialty coffees. Prices: ££. FSA hygiene rating: 5/5 (inspected 31 July 2024).

All four locations share a focus on authentic Thai cuisine and hold excellent food hygiene ratings (average 4.8/5). However, they should not be analysed as a single chain entity — there is no evidence of shared ownership, supply chain, branding, or marketing. The name "Thai Street Cafe" appears to be a generic descriptor rather than a protected brand.
