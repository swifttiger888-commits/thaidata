Overview

Rama Thai is a London-based Thai restaurant chain operating five locations across the capital: Stepney Green (Mile End Road), Camden, Norbury, Peckham, and Wandsworth. The chain was founded by Chef Maria, whose journey from Bangkok to London is central to the brand's story. Rather than traditional dine-in restaurants, Rama Thai operates primarily through a dark kitchen (cloud kitchen) model, partnering with kitchen providers like Encore Kitchens and Foodstars to prepare food for delivery via platforms such as Uber Eats, as well as for collection. This operational approach has allowed the chain to expand rapidly across London without the overheads of full-service restaurant premises.

The company behind the chain, RAMA THAI LIMITED, was incorporated on 19 December 2017 and remains active. Rama Thai positions itself as an authentic Thai experience offering home-style cooking with recipes passed down through generations. Menu prices are notably affordable for London, with mains such as Pad Thai and curries priced around £11-12, and meal deals offering further value. The chain has built a strong following on delivery platforms, boasting a 4.6/5 rating from over 10,000 reviews on Uber Eats. In December 2025, the brand opened its fifth kitchen location, signalling continued expansion.

Food hygiene ratings vary significantly across locations. The Peckham branch holds a top 5/5 rating, while Norbury and Wandsworth both score 4/5. However, the Stepney Green location scored only 2/5 ("Improvement necessary") at its most recent inspection in March 2026. Reviews consistently praise the food's authenticity, generous portions, and value for money, though some customers have noted occasional inconsistencies in quality.
