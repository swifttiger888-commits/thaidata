Overview

I-Chai is a Glasgow-based Chinese restaurant and takeaway chain that was established in 2005 in the Anniesland area of Glasgow's West End. Despite the user's request referencing Thai cuisine, I-Chai is firmly a Chinese cuisine operator. The business operates under a dual-format model: a full-service sit-down restaurant at 1015 Crow Road (Anniesland) alongside three "I-Chai Express" takeaway outlets located in Anniesland (944 Crow Road), Bishopbriggs (100 Auchinairn Road), and Erskine (Bridgewater Shopping Centre, PA8 7AA). This gives the chain a total of four UK locations, all concentrated in the Greater Glasgow area.

The chain positions itself as serving authentic Chinese cuisine with a modern twist, blending traditional cooking methods with contemporary innovation. Their menu spans starters, soups, chicken and beef dishes, satay favourites, and their popular chilli & salt creations. They offer multiple dining formats including an a la carte menu, unlimited tapas (available select days), and value lunch menus (2 courses for £12 / 3 courses for £13.50 as of recent menu data). I-Chai has been recognised with multiple awards including the Scottish Enterprise Award for "Best Chinese Cuisine" and the Glasgow Retail Business Award for "Best Eatery". On Dishcult, the Anniesland restaurant holds a strong 4.88 rating from nearly 2,000 reviews. Customer reviews frequently praise the generous portions, value for money, and family-friendly atmosphere.

In Scotland's Food Hygiene Information Scheme (FHIS), which uses a Pass/Improvement Required system rather than the 0-5 numerical scale used in England, all I-Chai locations inspected have achieved "Pass" ratings. The I-Chai Express (Anniesland) was inspected on 8 January 2024 and received a Pass; the main I-Chai restaurant (Anniesland) was inspected on 19 August 2025; the Bishopbriggs location was inspected on 7 October 2025 and received a Pass; and the Erskine location was inspected on 21 November 2024. The chain appears to be in a stable position with a slow, cautious expansion trajectory, having added Express takeaway locations over time while maintaining its original flagship restaurant. Their community engagement includes local sponsorships and partnerships, reinforcing their position as a neighbourhood fixture rather than a rapid-growth chain.
