Overview

Zaap Thai is a UK-based Thai street food restaurant chain founded by Yupha "Ban" Kaewkraikhot in 2015. The brand is part of the wider Sukho Group, which Ban co-founded with Gerrard Marks in 2002. The first Zaap restaurant opened on 18 May 2015 in Leeds city centre, followed by a second location in Nottingham later that year on 18 November 2015. The chain has since expanded to eight locations across the UK, including Leeds (two sites: city centre and Headingley), Nottingham, Newcastle, York, Sheffield, Durham, and Manchester (opened June 2025).

Zaap Thai's concept is inspired by the bustling street food markets of Bangkok. Each restaurant is designed to transport guests to Thailand through vibrant decor featuring neon lighting, custom-made tuk tuk seating booths, mock railway tracks, street-style graffiti, and eclectic Thai artwork. The menu features over 80 authentic Thai street food dishes including staples like Pad Thai, Som Tum salad, various curries, noodle soups, and stir-fries, alongside an extensive vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free offering. The chain operates a no-bookings, walk-in-only policy modelled on the informal street food culture of Thailand. Pricing is positioned in the affordable mid-range, with mains typically £12-£16, a £15 lunch set menu (Monday-Friday 12-5pm), and weekday daytime specials starting from £11.95. Bottomless brunch is also available at select locations for £35 per person.

The founder, Ban Kaewkraikhot, was born in Sukhothai, Thailand, and began her culinary journey helping her mother cook before moving to Bangkok at 16, where she ran a successful street food stall. After moving to the UK and working as a chef, she saved enough to open her first restaurant, Sukhothai, in 2002, later launching the Zaap brand to bring a more casual, street food-focused Thai dining experience to the UK market. In 2019, the business underwent a management buyout (MBO) facilitated by BDO, with the management team taking a shareholding alongside the founders, who remained active in the business.
