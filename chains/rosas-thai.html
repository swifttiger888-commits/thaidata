Rosa's Thai is the second-largest Thai restaurant chain in the ThaiData dataset, with 32 FSA-listed locations across 15 UK cities. All listed locations are dine-in restaurants. The chain is heavily concentrated in London (18 of 32 locations), with the remaining restaurants spread across England and one in Scotland.

City Presence

Rosa's Thai operates in 15 UK cities. London has the highest concentration by a significant margin:

  • London — 18 locations

Other cities with a Rosa's Thai location: Bath, Birmingham, Bristol, Dartford, Exeter, Glasgow, Guildford, Leeds, Liverpool, Manchester, Norwich, Oxford, Reading, Richmond upon Thames.
