Overview

Rosa's Thai Cafe is one of the UK's largest and most successful Thai restaurant chains, with 32 locations across England and Scotland as of May 2026. Founded by husband-and-wife team Saiphin and Alex Moore, the brand traces its origins to a market stall on Brick Lane, East London in 2007, before opening its first permanent restaurant on Hanbury Street, Spitalfields in 2008 — retaining the name "Rosa's" from the previous occupant due to limited funds for new signage. Co-founder and head chef Saiphin Moore grew up on a mountain farm in Khao Kho, northern Thailand, and every dish on the menu is created by her based on family recipes or inspirations from places she has lived.

The chain positions itself as a fast-casual, no-frills authentic Thai dining experience. Despite its scale, Rosa's maintains strong authenticity credentials: it imports around six shipping containers (151 tonnes) of rice annually from a factory in Phitsanulok province, Thailand, and sources 40,000kg of lemongrass and galangal directly from a family-owned farm in Pattalung province. Almost all dishes and drinks are priced under £10, making it one of the most affordable Thai chains in the UK. The brand has been recognised with multiple awards, including Eating Out Brand of the Year at the 2017 National Eat Out Awards, and was ranked third on MCA & AlixPartners' 2018 UK eating and drinking out growth tracker.

Rosa's has experienced rapid and sustained expansion, growing from a single East London site in 2008 to 13 sites by 2018, 28 by 2021, 32 by December 2022, 37 by September 2023, 40 by November 2023, and 47 by May 2026 — when it also secured a new £10.75m debt facility to support "ongoing and selective" expansion. The chain launched its first Scotland sites in Glasgow and Edinburgh in September 2023 and continues to target major UK cities outside London. In 2018, a majority stake was sold to American private equity firm TriSpan's Rising Stars platform, though founders Saiphin and Alex Moore remain involved, focusing on food quality and culture respectively.
