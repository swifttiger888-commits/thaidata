Overview

Banana Tree is a UK fast-casual Pan-Asian restaurant chain serving Southeast Asian street food-inspired dishes drawn from Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia and Singapore. Founded in the early 1990s by architect-turned-restaurateur William Chow as a small cafe called "Street Hawker" in Maida Vale, West London, the chain operated independently for nearly three decades before being acquired by The Big Table Group (operators of Las Iguanas, Bella Italia and Cafe Rouge) in September 2022. At acquisition, Banana Tree had 9 sites concentrated in London and the South East.

Under Big Table Group ownership, Banana Tree has experienced rapid national expansion, growing from 9 sites to approximately 22 locations by mid-2025. New openings since 2023 include Birmingham Bullring, Bath, Salford Quays (the brand's first North West location), Stevenage, Portsmouth Gunwharf Quays, and Cardiff (its largest site at 290 covers). The chain now operates in England and Wales across high-footfall locations including city centres, shopping districts and leisure parks. The brand is positioned as affordable fast-casual dining, with mains typically priced between £10-£15, a lunch deal under £11, and promotional offers including Bottomless Bowls and 2-for-1 cocktails. The menu features approximately 40% plant-based options, and the chain maintains a long-standing charity partnership with Orangutan Appeal UK.
