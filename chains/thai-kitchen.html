Overview

"Thai Kitchen" is not a single unified restaurant chain in the UK. Instead, it is the most commonly used naming pattern for independent Thai restaurants across the country. According to Food Standards Agency (FSA) data aggregated by Flux IoT, there are 155+ UK establishments with "Thai Kitchen" in their name, with an average food hygiene rating of 4.5 out of 5 and 53.85% holding a 5-star rating (as of June 2026). These are independently owned and operated businesses with no central ownership, franchise structure, or unified branding.

The closest example of a multi-location "Thai Kitchen" brand is the 101 Thai Kitchen group, a family-run collection of three restaurants tracing back to 2004 when they opened their first location in Hammersmith, West London (originally called 101 Thai Kitchen). In 2014, they opened Anissa's Thai Kitchen in the New Forest, and they currently operate Richmond Thai Kitchen in Richmond upon Thames. This group is notable for being pioneers of authentic Isaan (North-Eastern Thai) and Southern Thai cuisine in the UK - regional specialities rarely found in British Thai restaurants. Their family origins in Roi Et (Isaan region) and Hat Yai (Southern Thailand) inform a menu featuring dishes like som tum (papaya salad), larb (minced meat salad), and southern Thai curries that go far beyond standard pad thai and green curry offerings. Richmond Thai Kitchen holds a 5/5 FSA hygiene rating (inspected June 2025).

Another notable independent "Thai Kitchen" is the Thai Kitchen Authentic Street Food Cafe in Croydon (41 Whitehorse Road), a takeaway-focused operation with a 4/5 FSA hygiene rating (inspected May 2026), serving classic Thai street food at accessible prices (mains £8.95-£10.50). The broader "Thai Kitchen" landscape includes sit-down restaurants, takeaways, pub-kitchen operations (such as Thai Kitchen Saijai at the Wickham Arms), and delivery-focused outlets spanning every region of the UK.
