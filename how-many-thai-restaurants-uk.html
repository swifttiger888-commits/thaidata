Key Findings

  • 1 in 4 Thai restaurants in the UK is in London — 411 out of 1268
  • Giggling Squid is the largest named chain with 48 locations, followed by Rosa's Thai Cafe (32)
  • 56.7% of Thai restaurants hold a 5-star hygiene rating — far above the national average across all cuisines
  • Leeds (34) and Manchester (28) lead outside London
  • London has 411 Thai businesses across 32 boroughs — more than the next 10 cities combined
  • Scotland's 71 Thai businesses are now fully tracked via the FHIS scheme — Edinburgh leads with 24, Glasgow has 8
