About

Built for UK Thai restaurant operators

ThaiData UK is a free cost intelligence platform built specifically for Thai restaurant owners and operators in the United Kingdom. Our tools are designed to cut through the guesswork and give you the exact numbers you need to protect your margins — especially as the April 2026 National Living Wage increase puts pressure on every independent kitchen.

What we offer

  • Wage Calculator — Model your exact April 2026 staff cost increase across three age tiers in under 60 seconds
  • Thai Dish Price Checker — Search any dish (English or Thai script) for UK market pricing and true margin after delivery platform fees
  • Margin Stress Tester — Slide ingredient costs, labour, packaging, and commission to find your minimum viable menu price

Our data sources

All pricing data is sourced from UK-based Thai wholesale suppliers and menu surveys across Thai restaurant chains and independents.

  • Wholesale prices: JK Foods, Longdan, Thai Food Direct, Manning Impex, Midlands Food Service (Q1 2026)
  • Menu price benchmarks: Giggling Squid, Rose's Thai, Busaba Eathai, Thai Square, Patara, Mango Tree, regional independents
  • Minimum wage rates: UK Government official National Living Wage rates
  • Platform commissions: Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat operator terms (2026)

Why ThaiData?

Thai cuisine is the 4th fastest-growing cuisine in the UK — with over 4,000 Thai food businesses as of 2026. Yet most operators still price dishes from memory and discover their real margins only when cash flow becomes a problem. ThaiData exists to change that.

Everything on this site is free. No signup. No credit card. Just your numbers.

Who built this

ThaiData UK was built by Jonathan — UK Born Thai, UK-raised, with hands-on experience in hotel and restaurant operations. He previously worked with the Tourism Authority of Thailand (London) in IT and marketing, giving him a front-row view of the challenges facing Thai food businesses in the UK.

"Most small Thai restaurant owners are brilliant cooks but don't have time to crunch the numbers. They rely too heavily on delivery platforms and don't realise how much their online presence — Google Business, website rankings, even a basic menu price audit — affects their bottom line. ThaiData exists to change that."

— Jonathan, Founder
