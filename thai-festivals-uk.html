UK Thai Temple Directory

There are an estimated 35+ Thai Buddhist temples and meditation centres across the UK. Below are the major temples that host regular cultural events and festivals. Most welcome visitors — check opening times and dress modestly (covered shoulders and knees).

Wat Buddhapadipaวัดพุทธปทีป
14 Calonne Road, Wimbledon, London SW19 5HJ
🎉 The UK's first & largest Thai temple (est. 1965). Hosts UK's biggest Songkran and Loy Krathong. 60th anniversary in 2026. Open daily for visitors.
Wat Phra Singh UKวัดพระสิงห์
88 High Street, Runcorn, Cheshire WA7 1JH
🎉 Major northern temple. Songkran, Loy Krathong, regular meditation. Building a new Ubosot Hall — one of only 3 in the UK.
Wat Charoenbhavana (Manchester)วัดเจริญภาวนา
Gardner House, Cheltenham Street, Salford M6 6WY
🎉 First Thai temple in NW England. Dhammakaya tradition. Converted curtain-rail factory. Regular meditation and cultural events.
Wat Mahathat UKวัดมหาธาตุ
Eastfields House, Alrewas Road, Kings Bromley, DE13 7HR
🎉 Near Birmingham. Led by Ajahn Laow (3x royal appointee). Songkran, Loy Krathong, Kathina. Peaceful countryside setting.
WAT Buddharam Leedsวัดพุทธาราม
Leeds LS6 2ET
🎉 Serves the Yorkshire Thai community. Traditional ceremonies and cultural events.
Wat Phra Dhammakaya London
Woking, Surrey
🎉 Dhammakaya tradition. Modern facilities, frequent meditation retreats. Part of the global Dhammakaya network.
Wat Sanghapadipaวัดสังฆปทีป
Clase House, Pengwern Road, Swansea SA6 7LW
🎉 Wales' only Thai temple. Songkran and cultural events for the Welsh Thai community.
Wat Sangkhathan (Birmingham)
69 Coupers Road, Birmingham
🎉 One of the earliest Thai temples in the Midlands. Regular cultural events.
Wat Sriratanaram (Manchester)
Manchester
🎉 Serves the northern Thai community. Traditional Lanna-style ceremonies and events.
Wat Phra Dhammakaya Newcastle
Newcastle upon Tyne NE31
🎉 North East England's Dhammakaya centre. Active on Facebook: dmcnewcastle.
Wat Phra Dhammakaya Scotland
Helensburgh, Scotland
🎉 Scotland's primary Thai Buddhist centre. Dhammakaya tradition. Support groups in Doncaster & Sheffield.
Buddha Vihara Temple
Birmingham B6 6AN
🎉 Buddhist temple serving Birmingham's diverse Buddhist community including Thai practitioners.
