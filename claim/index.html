Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Claiming and managing your listing is completely free. We're building the most comprehensive directory of UK Thai restaurants.
What can I add to my listing?
Description, photos, website, social media links, menu highlights, opening hours, cuisine specialities, price range, and features like BYOB, delivery, vegan options, outdoor seating.
How long does verification take?
Usually within 48 hours. We verify you're genuinely associated with the restaurant before approving.
What if someone else claimed my restaurant?
Contact us at hello@thaidata.uk and we'll investigate. We may ask for proof of ownership.
