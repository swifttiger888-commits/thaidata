Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ThaiData UK ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at thaidata.uk. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect any information when you use our website, calculators and tools.

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal information. Specifically:

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily submit it via our email capture form to receive a free cost report. We never require an email to use the calculators.
  • Usage data — we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to collect anonymised data about how visitors interact with our site (pages viewed, time on site, click patterns, general location by country). No personally identifiable information is captured via GA4 or Clarity.
  • Calculator inputs — all inputs you enter into our calculators (staff numbers, dish names, cost sliders) are processed entirely in your browser. This data is never sent to our servers.
  • Claim submissions — if you submit a claim for a restaurant listing, we collect your name, email address and (optionally) your phone number so we can verify the claim and set up your listing.
  • Messages and correction reports — if you report a mistake on a listing or send us a message through the contact page, we store your message, the page it relates to (where applicable), your email address (only if you give one so we can reply) and your IP address (kept briefly, to block spam).

2. How we use your information

  • Email addresses are used solely to send the requested free cost report. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.
  • Analytics data is used to understand how users interact with the site and to improve our tools.

3. Cookies

We use cookies via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. These are analytics cookies used to distinguish users and sessions and understand user behaviour. You can manage your consent preferences via the cookie banner on our site. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or opt out of Clarity via Microsoft's privacy choices.

4. Data retention

Email addresses submitted via our form are retained until you request removal. Analytics data (GA4) is retained for 14 months. Microsoft Clarity retains session data for 13 months. You may request deletion of your email at any time by contacting us.

Messages, enquiries and correction reports are reviewed, acted on, and deleted within 12 months. IP addresses recorded to block spam are discarded within an hour.

5. Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@thaidata.uk.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4 — analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Clarity — analytics and heatmaps. Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for claim confirmations, magic links and report replies. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Telegram — internal admin notifications for new claims and correction reports. Telegram Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — font delivery. No personal data is logged by Google Fonts per their privacy FAQ.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact: hello@thaidata.uk
