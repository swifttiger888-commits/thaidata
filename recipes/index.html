Thai Recipes, From the Source

Most English-language Thai recipes are westernised beyond recognition. Every recipe here is researched from Thai-language sources — chefs, restaurants and home cooks in Thailand — translated, and cited. Where the sources disagree, we say so instead of hiding it.

The Recipes

Northern Thailand · Chiang Mai
Khao Soi
ข้าวซอย
Chiang Mai's curry noodle soup: egg noodles in a coconut-cream broth with a homemade spice paste, crowned with fried noodles. The dish TasteAtlas ranked the #1 soup in the world in 2022 — done the way northern Thailand actually makes it.
~65 min  ·  Serves 5  ·  6 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · Isaan & Central
Som Tum Thai
ส้มตำไทย
The classic green papaya salad, done properly: mortar-pounded with a four-flavour balance of sour, sweet, salty and hot. Recipe from a Michelin-recommended som tum chef — plus the whole som tum family mapped.
~15 min  ·  Serves 1 (doubles)  ·  5 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The One-Plate Meal
Pad Krapow
ผัดกะเพรา
Thailand's favourite one-plate meal: minced meat stir-fried at maximum heat with real holy basil, finished with a crispy runny-yolk egg over jasmine rice. Recipe from a Michelin-starred chef — plus the 1968 original.
~20 min  ·  Serves 1  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Nation's Favourite
Thai Green Curry
แกงเขียวหวาน
The curry Thais eat more than pad thai — from scratch. A 14-ingredient pounded paste from a Michelin-starred chef, cracked coconut cream, pea aubergines, and the green colour it's named for.
~1 hour  ·  Serves 3–4  ·  3 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Family-Table Soup
Tom Yum Goong
ต้มยำกุ้ง
The real hot and sour prawn soup: clear, sour-leading, lime squeezed off the heat, prawn-head stock. Recipe from a Michelin-starred chef — plus the four documented ways Thais make it creamy.
~45 min  ·  Serves 2–3  ·  3 cited Thai sources
 Isaan · The Guest-Honouring Dish
Laab
ลาบ
The real Isaan minced meat salad: khao khua toasted rice powder (with makrut leaves, the Bib Gourmand way), hot-salty-sour balance, fresh herbs matched to the meat.
~20 min  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Noodle Diplomat
Pad Thai
ผัดไทย
The dish the world knows, done right: tamarind-palm-sugar-fish-sauce balance, sen chan noodles, dried shrimp and preserved radish. No ketchup. Technique from Bib Gourmand chefs, plus the 3-star Chaiya variant.
~30 min  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The World's #1
Massaman Curry
แกงมัสมั่น
The curry CNN ranked the world's best food: a 16-ingredient spice paste — nutmeg, mace, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves — cracked coconut cream, potatoes and tamarind. Peanuts as a finish, never a sauce.
~80 min  ·  Serves 10 (halves)  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · Named After Its Herb
Tom Kha Gai
ต้มข่าไก่
The only Thai soup named after its ingredient: galangal poached gently in coconut milk, cream added late, lime squeezed in off the heat. No dairy, no ginger — Thai cooks mock the milk version in verse.
~35 min  ·  Serves 3–4  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Dish Three Countries Claim
Khao Man Gai
ข้าวมันไก่
Thai chicken rice: whole chicken poached and rubbed in its own rendered fat, rice stir-fried in that fat with ginger and garlic, and the tao jiao dipping sauce that makes it Thai.
~80 min  ·  Serves 4–6  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The World's #1 Stew
Panang Curry
พะแนง
The curry TasteAtlas ranked #1 stew: beef braised in coconut milk, a from-scratch paste, ground peanuts stirred into the curry as it reduces, pickled shallots on the side. No lime.
~90 min  ·  Serves 4  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Chef-Killer Dish
Pad See Ew
ผัดซีอิ๊ว
Fresh wide rice noodles pre-coated in dark soy and charred in a very hot wok — the burnt edges are the point — with ice-water-marinated pork, egg and kale.
~25 min + marinating  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Dish Everyone Thinks They Can Cook
Khao Pad
ข้าวผัด
Day-old jasmine rice fried hard in a hot wok, garlic and pork and egg, tomato folded in gently, prik nam pla on the side. Plus the crab version that put a street stall in the Michelin Guide.
~20 min  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Coachella Dessert
Mango Sticky Rice
ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง
Steamed glutinous rice folded hot with salted coconut cream and pandan, ripe mango, salted cream sauce, fried mung beans. The salt is the dish — the first dessert on the hub.
~4 hrs incl. soak  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Sour Curry With No Orange
Gaeng Som
แกงส้ม
The sour curry named for its sourness: prawn meat pounded into the paste, tamarind and lime together, prawns curled to a C and no further. Southern turmeric version documented.
~40 min  ·  Serves 3–4  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Dish That's Better Tomorrow
Khao Kha Mu
ข้าวขาหมู
Pork leg fried until the skin tightens, braised in palm-sugar caramel with cinnamon and star anise, rested overnight and reheated. Pickled mustard and sour chilli vinegar on the side.
~16 hrs incl. rest  ·  Serves 6–8  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Salad Where the Dressing Is Everything
Yum Neua
ยำเนื้อย่าง
Grilled sirloin, rested and sliced against the grain, dressed warm with a pounded lime-fish-chilli paste balanced in the bowl before it touches the meat. The bitter-lime lesson included.
~25 min  ·  Serves 2  ·  4 cited Thai sources
 Thailand · The Noodle Bowl That Isn't a Soup
Tom Yum Noodle Soup
ก๋วยเตี๋ยวต้มยำ
Clear pork-bone stock with pickled garlic, dressed per bowl with ground peanuts, chilli flakes, fish sauce and lime — just enough liquid to coat. The Sukhothai war documented.
~2 hrs  ·  Serves 4  ·  4 cited Thai sources

How These Recipes Are Sourced

  • Thai-language sources first. Chefs, restaurants, cooking schools and home cooks in Thailand — not English re-interpretations of them.
  • Every recipe is cited. Each page lists its sources with links and access dates.
  • Disagreements are documented. Thai cooks differ — turmeric root vs powder, ginger vs galangal. We show the variations instead of pretending one recipe is canonical.
  • Western substitutions are called out. Where UK recipes cut corners, we name it.
