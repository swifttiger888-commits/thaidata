Eligibility Criteria

Chef roles and the 22 July 2025 rule change: chefs are SOC 5434, an RQF Level 3 occupation. New Skilled Worker applications now require RQF Level 6 unless the occupation is on the Immigration Salary List or Temporary Shortage List — chefs are on neither, so chefs can no longer be newly sponsored, whatever the salary. Only chefs whose first certificate of sponsorship predates 22 July 2025 can extend or switch sponsor. Cooks (SOC 5435) were never eligible.

UK Thai Chef Visa FAQ — 2026 Context

What is the true 2026 cost to sponsor a Thai chef in the UK?

Beyond salary, sponsors face CoS fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) which is £3,105 for 3 years in 2026, and potential sponsor license costs ranging from £536 to £1,476.

How does the £12.71 NLW affect the Skilled Worker threshold?

The 2026 National Living Wage of £12.71/hr effectively pushes the floor for Skilled Worker salaries, as the "Going Rate" for SOC 5434 (Chefs) must exceed this to satisfy both labor and immigration law.

Can a Thai chef be sponsored under the Skilled Worker visa?

Not as a new hire since 22 July 2025. Chefs are SOC 5434 (RQF Level 3) and are on neither the Immigration Salary List nor the Temporary Shortage List, so new sponsorships stopped when the standard route moved to RQF Level 6 roles. Only chefs whose first certificate of sponsorship predates 22 July 2025 can extend or switch sponsor. Cooks (SOC 5435) were never eligible. Verify: gov.uk eligible occupations.

2026 salary thresholds that matter

From 22 July 2025: general Skilled Worker threshold £41,700 (up from £38,700); new entrants £33,400; transitional extensions £31,300 before 1 December 2026. Pay must also meet the occupation's going rate (chefs: £33,400 standard, £25,000 new entrant). Verify: gov.uk going rates.
