    Local AI Visibility FAQ

    Why is NAP consistency critical for 2026 AI search?

    Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 Pro use 'triangulation' to verify business facts. If your phone number on Yelp differs from your Google profile, the AI loses trust and may exclude you from its recommendations.

    What is a 'Neural Trust Score'?

    Our proprietary score calculates how easily an automated AI agent can verify your business name, address, and phone (NAP) without encountering data conflicts.

    Does having multiple locations hurt my local SEO?

    Only if they are not properly distinguished. Each location must have a unique, consistent NAP fingerprint across all 50+ directories to maintain separate entity authority.

    How often should I audit my business citations?

    We recommend a quarterly audit to catch 'data decay' where directories update their listings with incorrect scraped information from third-party sources.
